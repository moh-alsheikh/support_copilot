Lumen Refund And Support Policy

Refund window: full refunds are available within 30 days of the original purchase date for annual
plans, and within 14 days for monthly plans.

Support response time: paid plans receive a first response within 1 business day. Free plan
support is best-effort through the community forum only.

Data retention after cancellation: files remain stored for 90 days after subscription
cancellation, then are permanently deleted.

Account deletion: deleting a Lumen account is permanent after a 30-day grace period and cannot be
reversed once the grace period ends.
