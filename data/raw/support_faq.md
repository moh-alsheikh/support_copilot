# Lumen Support FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the email link. Reset links expire
after 24 hours.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Account > Billing > Cancel Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing period; you keep access until then.

Q: Can I change my billing cycle from monthly to annual?
A: Yes, from Account > Billing > Change Plan. The change applies immediately and you are credited
for unused time on the monthly plan.

Q: What happens to my files if I downgrade from Pro to Free?
A: Files already uploaded are kept, but new uploads pause once you exceed the Free plan's 5 GB
limit until you free up space or upgrade again.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Email support@lumen.example or use the in-app chat. Paid plans receive a response within 1
business day.
